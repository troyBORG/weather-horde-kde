# Holiday coverage

Scope: complete recurring federal public holidays and Iowa Code 2026 Chapter 1C date provisions; curated, NOT exhaustive US observances. Statutory recognition is not proof of an annual proclamation or a paid day off.

Arbor Week uses Sunday–Saturday containing Arbor Day; statute does not specify the week start. Disabled by default. Federal actual and paid observed dates are separate occurrences; Iowa legal-only days are not weekend-shifted.

## Exclusions
- Inauguration Day: limited DC employee scope, not national
- Indigenous Peoples' Day: jurisdiction/proclamation not verified
- Easter and optional faith packs (Hanukkah, Passover, Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Ramadan/Eids, Diwali, Lunar New Year, Kwanzaa): date methods/boundaries not verified; inactive
- Iowa statehood, Beggars' Night, state fair, municipal and one-time proclamations: not verified
